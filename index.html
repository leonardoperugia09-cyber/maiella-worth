Abruzzo, on Wandsworth Road

The Maiella is the mountain that watches over Abruzzo. We cook the way its villages do: chitarra cut on the wire, lamb skewered by hand, and Montepulciano in the glass.

Spaghetti alla chitarra takes its name from the guitar: a wooden frame strung with wire that cuts the sheet of dough into square strands.

Arrosticini, by the dozen

Small cubes of sheep, threaded on wooden skewers and grilled hard, the way shepherds on the Maiella have eaten them for centuries.

They come wrapped in foil in a terracotta pot, with focaccia baked here. Order by the skewer or pick a set.
